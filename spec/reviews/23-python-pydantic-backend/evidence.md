# Evidence-method review

## Summary

The issue #23 slice carries 127 advisor-visible obligations — 110 acceptance
criteria across FR-072..FR-080, NFR-026 and NFR-027, plus the 17 NFR metric
rows — 30 `Constraints` rows, and 100 matrix rows at TC-845..944. `quoin advise
--json` (2026-09-04) placed every one of them: **zero inconclusive**, 8
mismatches, 28 uncatalogued. The advisor does not model the 30 `Constraints`
rows; every `Validation` cell on those rows was judged here, and that judgement
is labelled as such.

All 8 mismatches are the lexical pattern SR-052 recorded at FND-802 and SR-069
at its Summary: `property_shapes=example|universal` recommends a Test method
for an obligation authored `Analysis`. In this slice all eight — FR-074-AC-10,
FR-076-AC-12, FR-077-AC-10, FR-079-AC-7, NFR-026-AC-9, NFR-027-AC-4,
NFR-027-AC-5, NFR-027-AC-10 — are branch-diff or module-scan claims correctly
paired `Analysis` + `Static`, and only the last is worth a row (its matrix row
is `Integration`, not `Static`). They are recorded at FND-1154 so they are not
re-raised, not raised as findings.

The residue is where the work is, and it lands on the three things the slice
exists to prove. **The injection guard, the semantic-loss inspection, and the
falsification gates are the slice's three load-bearing oracles, and none of the
three is given a method that can discriminate what it claims.** FR-075-AC-1 and
FR-075-AC-5 check "at any depth … including every applicator" against four
enumerated positions; FR-078-AC-4 checks "any nesting depth inside an annotation
expression" against three enumerated shapes (FND-1144). The two provisioning
falsifications — every gate fails rather than skips, every guard fails rather
than asserting over an empty set — are bundled into `Static` rows that cannot
fail for them (FND-1145). And the three obligations whose whole purpose is to
prove the gates can go red (FR-078-AC-10, FR-080-AC-5, FR-080-AC-9) name no
mutant mechanism, and two of the three mutate artefacts that `--check` gates in
the same slice freeze (FND-1146).

Below that: eleven `Verification` cells carry an evidence *kind* where the
catalog expects a method *class*; seven `Test` cells are discharged by `Static`
rows; the same determinism shape is typed three ways; the two `Fuzz` rows are a
fixed regression corpus with no generator; two Security constraints are traced
to rows whose text does not mention them; and the slice contains no `Manual`
row at all although two of its obligations are irreducibly human.

Nothing in this slice matched `fault-detection-unmeasured` or
`fault-detection-failed`: TC-845..944 are all `🚧 in progress`, so no obligation
is bound to a run yet. That is expected, not a gap. No spec artefact was edited
by this review and nothing under `conformance/` or `python_backend/` was read
or changed.
