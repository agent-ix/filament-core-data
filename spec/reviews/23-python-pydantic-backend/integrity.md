# Integrity review

## Summary

US-013 is elaborated by FR-072..FR-080 and constrained by NFR-026 and NFR-027
under StR-001. Every acceptance criterion and every named constraint reaches a
case in TC-845..944, the ids are unique, and no scoped artifact is orphaned.
The bundle is not yet single-interpretation. Six defects are structural. The
qualification vocabulary is not closed: FR-077 declares three verdicts,
`spec/tests.md` uses two different spellings and a fourth value, FR-073 adds a
`runtimeValidation` disposition and FR-080 adds a "validating family", and no
requirement says which of the four decides. The measurement is pre-empted: the
equivalence-class table already records the verdict and the retained and lost
constructs of all five families, which FR-077-CON-2 and US-013-EX-2 forbid.
FR-078 fails generation on a degraded annotation with no per-family exemption,
so the families FR-077-AC-6 and FR-080-AC-5 must measure and falsify can never
be generated, and FR-078-AC-3 cannot be satisfied for them. FR-079 emits no
package for a `not-qualified` family while FR-080-AC-1 and FR-080-AC-6 type-check
and record exactly those families, over a tree that does not exist, and the term
"demonstrated profile" that decides which packages exist is never defined.
FR-074-AC-3's oracle is already dead: FR-073-AC-3 forces `--extra-fields forbid`
on every Pydantic profile, and the committed spike golden shows `extra='forbid'`
produced from the *unprepared* `input.schema.json`, so the closure rewrite this
requirement exists for is unobservable in the family it is demonstrated on.
FR-074 also applies FR-043's normalizer, which is defined over the official
TypeSpec bundle and stamps a spike-specific `urn:` `$id`, to published
`schema/semantic/v1/*.schema.json` documents. Five medium findings are
unfalsifiable and vacuous criteria, one obligation stated six times across five
artifacts, three "declared" limits nothing declares, and constraints whose only
mapped case asserts a different obligation. No spec artifact was edited by this
review.
