# Evidence-method review

## Summary

The issue #36 slice carries 136 obligations: 103 acceptance criteria and 33
constraints across FR-091..099 and NFR-031..033, planned against TC-1200..1329.
Every obligation names a method and a TC id; none is `TBD`. The tooling the
methods assume was checked against the tree and the host: `cargo-deny 0.19.8`,
`cargo-audit 0.22.1`, and `rustup` toolchain `1.98.1-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu`
are installed but no `Makefile` target runs any of them; `ix-trace-rs` tag
`v0.1.1` exists; `node src/compiler/cli.mjs inspect --ir` and `generate
--target` exist; `agent_ix_semantic_ir::decide` returns `result_state`,
`diagnostics`, and a `normalized` canonical string; `test/changed-paths.ts`
exports `changeRange`; `unshare` is on the host. The matrix (`spec/tests.md`)
tops out at TC-1108, so no TC-12xx/13xx row exists yet.

Two obligations cannot be produced as written: NFR-031-AC-4 needs an
enumeration seam that FR-091-CON-2 forbids and quire-rs does not expose, and
NFR-031-AC-5's `std::fs` allowlist excludes the writer FR-097 requires. Seven
determinism obligations judge the serializer by running it twice, one of them
labelled `Snapshot` with no baseline. One AC has no falsifying control. Nine
obligations authored `Test` are Make/git/toolchain rehearsals no `cargo test`
can produce and name no artifact. The #36 AC-5 gap is stated honestly in
FR-098 but its proxy row must not be traced to AC-5.
