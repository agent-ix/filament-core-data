# Integrity review

## Summary

US-011 is elaborated by FR-054..062 and constrained by NFR-022..023 under
StR-001. Coverage is mechanically complete and was computed from the files, not
asserted: the eleven new requirements declare 101 acceptance criteria, 39 named
constraints and 15 NFR criteria — 155 obligations — and every one of them is
named by at least one of the 100 rows TC-645..744, with no id gap, no duplicate,
and no criterion cited that no requirement declares. The execution summary
arithmetic also holds: the per-method deltas (Unit +43, Integration +20,
Analysis +17, Static +7, Property +6, Snapshot +3, Compile +2, Fuzz +2) sum to
100 and reconcile 644 → 744 mapped, 642 passed, 102 blocked. `spec.md` §2.1,
§2.2 and §5, `index.md` and `log.md` all carry the issue #21 delta consistently,
and the id allocation (US-011, FR-054..062, NFR-022..023, TC-645..744, SR-078)
collides with nothing in the repository.

FR-057's central claim was checked empirically rather than read. A transcription
of the five-clause "declared language of the published locus path pattern" was
differentially tested under Node against
`^(?!/)(?![A-Za-z]:)(?!.*\\)(?!.*(?:^|/)\.\.(?:/|$))[^\u0000]+$` over every
string of length 0..5 across a ten-character alphabet including `/`, `\`, `.`,
`:`, U+000A, U+000D and U+2028: **zero mismatches**. The stated language is the
ECMA-262 language, GAP-002's resolution is sound as written, and the head-based
line-terminator reading is correct. The probe alphabet FR-057-AC-6 *declares*,
however, cannot detect the defect that resolution exists to prevent (FND-907).

The record is not yet single-interpretation. Four high findings are
contradictions rather than omissions: `Option<Nullable<T>>` under derived serde
collapses the very distinction FR-054-AC-4 asserts; FR-059's own output set
writes a path NFR-023 prohibits and FR-059-AC-12 forbids; FR-059-AC-6 consumes a
case member no published corpus case carries and that this issue may not add;
and the published `rust` target contract declares `AGPL-3.0-or-later` where the
issue, the repository and FR-056-CON-4 all say `AGPL-3.0-only`, with no
requirement reconciling the two. Eight medium findings are undefined inputs (the
`bytes` wire form, `SemanticIdentity`, the doc-comment source), an unstated
tie-break (the `enum`/`union` row predicate), a criterion whose arithmetic does
not close, a probe alphabet that cannot falsify, a dependency edge stated in two
directions, and a resolved gap with nowhere to land. No spec artifact, source
file, fixture, schema or conformance file was edited by this review.

| FND-900 | high | The three-axis composition does not distinguish the four presence×nullable states on the wire, which is the one thing FR-054 invents `Nullable<T>` to do. The axes compose collection, then nullability, then presence, so an optional nullable field is `Option<Nullable<T>>`, and FR-054 specifies nothing else — no `#[serde(default)]`, no `skip_serializing_if`, no `deserialize_with`. Under serde's derived implementation a JSON `null` deserializes `Option<X>` through `visit_none`/`visit_unit` to `None`, exactly as an absent member does, so present-null and absent both arrive as `None` and the inner `Nullable::Null` is unreachable from JSON; symmetrically, `None` serializes as `null`, so an absent member re-serializes as a present `null`. FR-054-AC-4 requires the two to be "distinguishable" *and* to "round-trip to the bytes they came from", and EC-69 names the collapse as the risk. As written both halves fail on the specified attribute set, and TC-648 cannot be authored to pass. Resolve by specifying the member-level serde treatment for the optional-nullable composition (a `default` plus a `deserialize_with` that distinguishes missing from null, plus `skip_serializing_if` on the absent arm), and by stating it in the axis table rather than leaving it to the emitter. | FR-054 Behavior "Fields", FR-054-AC-3, FR-054-AC-4, FR-054-CON-1, TC-647, TC-648, EC-69, EC-71 |

| FND-907 | medium | FR-057-AC-6's declared probe alphabet cannot falsify the validator it exists to prove.
