# Base specification review

## Summary

The issue #21 specification adds one user story, nine functional requirements,
two non-functional requirements, and 100 test cases covering the IR-to-Rust
mapping, identifier derivation, crate emission, constraint and pattern
enforcement, the closed diagnostic registry, the `rust-backend` conformance
adapter and its independent Rust reader, determinism and the support matrix,
the two consumers, and the mapping-branch register.

Seven analyses ran in parallel subagents against the first draft and returned
**91 findings: 35 high, 47 medium, 9 low**. Every one is
dispositioned in the table below. Two are recorded as not defects, with the
evidence that disproves them. The rest are fixed in the specification or
accepted with a named reason, and five are fixed as far as this branch may go
and filed as issues against the artifact owners, because the artifacts they
touch are prohibited paths here.

Three findings deserve the reader's attention before the rest.

**GAP-002 is answered, and answering it found a second defect.** The published
`sourceLocus.path` pattern uses four ECMAScript lookaheads that no RE2-family
engine compiles, which is why the corpus registered the `rust-backend` slot
`unavailable` and budgeted it one divergence. FR-057 answers it with a
hand-written validator admitted only through a differential equivalence harness.
The language the published pattern denotes was characterised in prose and then
*measured* against Node's `RegExp`, reading the pattern out of
`schema/semantic/v1/common.schema.json` rather than retyping it: zero
disagreements over 149,899 exhaustive strings across three alphabets plus
200,000 seeded random strings. The measurement also showed that the pattern's
traversal, UNC and absolute-path guards do not apply past a line terminator,
because ECMAScript `.` cannot cross one — `a<LF>../../etc/passwd` validates. That
is a security-relevant defect in a published schema, it is not what GAP-002
records, and it is filed as issue #56. FR-057 therefore exports two predicates:
the published language, and a separately named `is_traversal_free` for the
intended one, with the divergence recorded rather than silently resolved. The
divergence budget goes unspent.

**Two wire forms are refused rather than invented.** No published artifact
states the JSON representation of the `bytes` kernel scalar or the discriminator
of a `union`. The first is refused with a blocking `UNDECLARED_WIRE_FORM`; the
second takes serde's external tagging, which is the only form needing no
invented name, and is recorded as this backend's stated reading. Both are filed
as issue #58, because issues #22 and #23 must reach the same bytes for the issue
#7 parity gate. Deciding a cross-language wire form inside one backend is the
failure the corpus exists to catch.

**The ticket's fourth acceptance criterion closes partly unmet, and the record
says so.** "Two clean runs are byte-identical across supported platforms" needs
a second platform. The repository's only CI is a Node-only reusable workflow on
`workflow_dispatch` with no Rust toolchain, and `.github/**` is prohibited to
this change. FR-060 therefore lists exactly one platform row as supported with
named measured evidence and records every other row unmet with its reason and
its owning issue, and NFR-022's metric is restated so it cannot be satisfied by
declining to measure. Issue #60 owns the gap. This is the same shape issue #42
already records, and it is stated rather than construed as satisfied.

Two structural changes moved ids and are called out because a reader will
notice them: the criterion set grew from 116 to 148 acceptance criteria and from
39 to 56 named constraints as the findings were applied, so the coverage tables
were remapped over the same TC block (TC-645..744) rather than by minting new
rows; and six diagnostic codes were added to FR-058's registry —
`UNDECLARED_WIRE_FORM`, `UNSUPPORTED_MULTIPLICITY`,
`UNKNOWN_POLICY_ON_NON_RECORD`, `PAYLOAD_ON_ENUM_VARIANT`, `UNORDERED_SUBJECT`,
`INVALID_DEFAULT_VALUE`, `UNSAFE_OUTPUT_ROOT` — each because a construct that
had no disposition now has a named refusal instead of silence.
